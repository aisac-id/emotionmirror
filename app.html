    How EmotionMirror works

    Engine. MediaPipe FaceLandmarker runs on-device (CPU, WASM) and outputs 52 ARKit-style blendshapes — tiny facial-action scores like mouthSmileLeft or browDownRight.

    Emotions. A transparent rule engine condenses them into 13 features and scores 7 expressions (joy, sadness, anger, surprise, fear, disgust, neutral). Softmax turns evidence into the spectrum you see; a 4-frame stability gate prevents flicker.

    Affect pad. Valence (unpleasant ↔ pleasant) and arousal (calm ↔ activated) place your expression on the classic circumplex map.

    Mood index. A time-weighted moving average of valence (0–100). The session report accumulates dwell time per emotion, transitions, longest streak and blinks.

    Demo. A synthetic face cycles through all expressions with ground-truth labels so you can verify the engine without a camera.

    Privacy. Video never leaves this page. No network, no recording — only settings and your neutral baseline are stored locally.

    This is a wellness toy, not a medical or psychological instrument. Blendshape-based expression reading is approximate and culture-dependent.
